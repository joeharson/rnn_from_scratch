Lesson 1

1. Machine learning is the study of algorithms that improve through experience.
2. Deep learning uses neural networks to learn patterns from data.
3. A recurrent neural network processes information one step at a time.
4. The hidden state carries information from previous time steps.
5. Backpropagation through time computes gradients across an entire sequence.
6. Gradient clipping helps prevent exploding gradients during training.
7. Softmax converts logits into probabilities that sum to one.
8. Cross entropy measures how well predicted probabilities match the correct target.
9. Careful debugging is as important as writing new code.
10. Good datasets are clean, consistent, and representative of the task.
11. Experiments should be repeatable with fixed random seeds.
12. Learning requires observation, iteration, and patience.
13. Every training run teaches something about the model or the data.
14. Small improvements in preprocessing can noticeably improve results.
15. Engineers inspect losses, gradients, and generated samples regularly.
16. Understanding fundamentals makes advanced models easier to learn.
17. LSTMs and GRUs were designed to reduce the vanishing gradient problem.
18. Transformers later replaced recurrent networks in many language tasks.
19. Even so, RNNs remain an excellent way to understand sequence modeling.
20. Practice builds intuition better than memorizing formulas.
In lesson 1, the focus is on building reliable AI systems through careful implementation, testing, and continuous improvement.
